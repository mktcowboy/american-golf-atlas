# American Golf Atlas

Interactive map of notable U.S. golf courses — state and regional leaderboards cross-referenced with national Top 100 lists.

**[Live map](https://mktcowboy.github.io/american-golf-atlas/)** · **[Top 100 history](https://mktcowboy.github.io/american-golf-atlas/history.html)**

## What’s included

- Map with imagery, dark, and light basemaps (free tiles; no API keys)
- Filters for ★ Top 100 USA, Best in State (#1 per state), national lists, and regional chips (e.g. California Top 100, Texas Top 50)
- Ranked table with CSV export and a map-only / hide-panels mode
- Separate history archive of Golf Digest and GOLF Magazine Top 100 editions

The map always reflects **current** editions (Golf Digest 2025–26, GOLF Magazine 2024–25). Prior years live on the history page only.

## Sources

Compiled for personal research from public rankings (Top100GolfCourses, Golf Digest, GOLF Magazine, Golfweek) and open geographic data. Not for commercial redistribution of ranking content without permission from the publishers.
